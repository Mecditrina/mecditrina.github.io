Exploring the mechanical forces of life

A regional network giving researchers access to mechanobiology tools, skills and devices to measure and model mechanical forces in biology and soft matter, with support from expert physicists and mechanobiologists in Marseille and Nice.

Request access Explore the instruments

A service for researchers

MECDITRINA links the mechanobiology expertise already present across the region into a single entry point. You bring the biological question; we help you find the right technique, the right experts and the right way to analyse the data.

Tools and devices

  • Force measurements at different scales
  • Adapted to diverse samples
  • Various experimental conditions and environments

Expertise and support

  • Experimental design
  • Technical advice
  • Help with measurements
  • Tailored approaches and training

Data analysis and modelling

  • Image and force analysis
  • Quantification
  • Theoretical modelling
  • Interpretation

A wide range of samples

  • Proteins · purified, molecular complexes
  • Cells · adherent, suspended, immune, stem cells
  • Tissues and organs · 3D organoids, ex vivo tissues
  • Histology · tissue sections
  • Membranes · model membranes, lipid rafts, cell membranes
  • ECM and biomaterials · hydrogels, alginate beads, 3D matrices

How it works

One entry point for every request, whatever the technique or the site.

  1. Submit your requestDescribe your question and samples through the online form.
  2. We review itThe project engineer analyses each request within 2–5 working days.
  3. We connect youYou are routed to the right expert and technique, after steering-committee advice for complex projects.
  4. First dataExperiments start with the expert team, usually yielding first data within 1–3 months, with training towards autonomy.

Two hubs, one network

Marseille

DyNaMo — Luminy campus

Quantitative biophysics, with a strong focus on atomic force microscopy: cell and tissue mechanics, high-speed AFM, single-molecule and single-cell force spectroscopy.

Nice

IRCAN — Nice

Cell-based mechanobiology: traction force microscopy, cell stretching and magnetic tweezers.

About the name

Mecditrina refers to Meditrina, a Roman goddess associated with healing, in line with the project's ambition to contribute to new approaches against cancer and other diseases. MECDITRINA is a structuring action of Canceropôle PACA (October 2026 – June 2028).
