Instruments

The MECDITRINA technology portfolio, across the Marseille and Nice sites.

The portfolio gathers validated techniques already in routine use in the partner laboratories. Access is either through direct collaboration with the expert team or through the existing core facilities. Not sure which technique fits your question? Send us a request: we will help you choose.

Mechanical characterisation across scales

Elasticity and viscoelasticity of samples from nanometre to centimetre scales, under physiological conditions.

Marseille Nice

AFM nanoindentation and microrheology

Elasticity and viscoelasticity of membranes, vesicles, bacteria, cells and tissues; high-resolution topographical imaging.

Marseille: Bruker NanoWizard 4 and 5 on fluorescence microscopes (TIRF, spinning disk). Nice: AFM available, upgrade planned.

Marseille

High-speed AFM

Dynamic imaging of biomolecules and membranes at the nanoscale.

RIBM PS-NEX high-speed AFM.

Marseille Under deployment

AFM coupled to TIRF microscopy

Simultaneous force measurement and near-membrane fluorescence imaging.

Cell–matrix interactions and force generation

Engineered substrates to measure how cells pull on and respond to their microenvironment.

Nice Marseille

Traction force microscopy (TFM)

Quantitative vector maps of the forces exerted by single cells or groups of cells on their substrate.

Fully operational in Nice; being developed in Marseille.

Marseille

Engineered substrates and micropatterning

Polyacrylamide hydrogels with user-defined stiffness, controlled matrix composition (collagen, fibronectin…), micropatterned adhesive geometries.

Mechanical stimulation and force application

Controlled mechanical perturbation to study mechanotransduction.

Nice

Cyclic uniaxial cell stretching

Cell responses to stretch with tunable frequency and amplitude.

Nice

Magnetic tweezers

Localised force application to membrane receptors or intracellular structures through magnetic beads.

Marseille Under development

Compression

Cell and tissue responses to compressive stress.

Marseille Under development

Optical tweezers

Calibrated pico- to nanonewton forces for cell–matrix and cell–cell interactions and active microrheology in 3D matrices and organoids.

Molecular-scale force probing and affinity

From single-molecule unbinding forces to cell–cell adhesion.

Marseille

Single-molecule force spectroscopy (AFM)

Unfolding and unbinding forces of molecular interactions.

Marseille

Single-cell force spectroscopy

Cell–cell and cell–ligand adhesion forces, detachment work and interaction dynamics.

Marseille

Surface plasmon resonance (SPR)

Binding affinities and kinetics, for example antibody–ligand interactions.

Data analysis software

AFM mechanical measurements are analysed with PyFMLab, open-source software developed at DyNaMo for elasticity and viscoelasticity of biological samples (Lopez-Alonso et al., Open Res Eur 2023).
