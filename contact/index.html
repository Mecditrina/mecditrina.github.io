Request access to an instrument or to expertise, or simply ask a question.

How to request access

  1. Fill in the formYour question, your samples and the technique you have in mind, if any.
  2. Review within 2–5 working daysThe project engineer reads every request and comes back to you.
  3. RoutingSimple requests are routed within a week; complex ones are discussed by the steering committee (2–3 weeks).
  4. StartYou are put in touch with the expert team to plan the experiments and, if relevant, training.
